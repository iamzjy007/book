只翻译末尾待译区。下列候选由英文触发，先核对语境再采用。本次作品代号为 lori。带篇号的约定按作品和适用范围使用。

## 必读要点

信优先于达，达优先于雅。用现代简体中文保留和合本式的庄重和朗读节奏。辨明说话者与受话者，随原文切换我、我们、你和你们。保留条件、否定、时间、确定程度、重复与比喻。避免仿古虚词，勿借熟悉的经句增添内容。

## 本篇用词提示

- **异版经文直引**　已引和合本且经节与引文范围相符的，保留既有中文。AMPC、NKJV 等是英文来源标识。审校先核中文经文；遇到实质性版本差异再单独处理，旁引可保留在出处或译注。　适用范围：明确引用经文；仅化用时，保留作者的人称、所指和引述范围。（用户复评／Lori 131、132）
- **无需害怕**　这是安慰，可译“你们没有什么可怕的／用不着害怕”。　适用范围：与描述已经无所畏惧、或直接命令不要害怕分辨。（编辑审校／Lori 122、126、128）

## 和合本惯用词与短语

先核原句所指，再取相合的措辞。人称、动作和修饰语以本篇英文为准。

- armored up / My armor → **军装**（可取，以弗所书 6:11）。神所赐的争战装备；所有格随说话人，只有 whole/full 才添“全副”，armored up 要译出穿戴。 同书现译同段见「军装」3段（如047篇第1段）。

- outpouring of My Spirit → **将我的灵浇灌**（可取，使徒行传 2:17）。作者另列荣耀等受浇灌之物，照样译出。 同书现译同段见「将我的灵浇灌」4段（如032篇第3段）、「倾注」1段（如089篇第5段）、「浇灌」1段（如132篇第4段）。

- power from on high → **从上头来的能力**（可取，路加福音 24:49）。特指从神而来的能力；原文仅提能力时，不添领受或等候。 同书现译同段见「从上头来的能力」10段（如052篇第7段）。

- work for good → **得益处**（可取，罗马书 8:28）。保留作者给出的受益者和条件；不添回经文中作者未用的限定。

- setting captives free → **被掳的得释放**（可取，路加福音 4:18）。“使被掳的得释放”贴近和合本；“释放被掳的人”语义通顺，可回看句势，proclaim 则译“宣告／报告被掳的得释放”。

- signs and wonders → **神迹奇事**（可取，使徒行传 5:12）。依作者次序先译神迹，再译奇事；若另列 miracles，保留三项。 同书现译同段见「神迹奇事」18段（如035篇第6段）、「神迹、奇事」11段（如034篇第3段）、「神迹和奇事」2段（如064篇第3段）。

- day of vengeance → **报仇的日子**（可取，以赛亚书 61:2）。原文若未点明报仇者，勿补主语。

- clothed in righteousness → **披上公义**（可取，诗篇 132:9）。只说穿戴公义时译“披上公义”；原文另提 robe 才具体说袍。

- commit your ways → **将你的事交托**（可取，诗篇 37:5）。神作受托者时可说“将你们的事交托给我”；不逐字造出“交托道路”。

- hiding place → **藏身之处**（可取，诗篇 32:7）。神作藏身之处，或神为人预备藏身之处，随原文区分。

- anointing → **恩膏**（可取，约翰一书 2:27）。所领受的膏；作及物动词时取“膏”。 同书现译同段见「恩膏」21段（如011篇第4段）、「膏抹」2段（如094篇第3段）。

- glory → **荣耀**（可取，提摩太后书 4:18）。尊荣归给神，或神彰显荣耀。若有光照、面容发光的描写，可另辨“荣光”。 同书现译同段见「荣耀」66段（如001篇第4段）。

其余有语境依据的用词：Victory → 得胜（哥林多前书 15:57）；promises → 应许（彼得后书 1:4）；praise → 赞美（路加福音 19:37）；worship → 敬拜（启示录 22:9）；ambassadors → 使者（哥林多后书 5:20）；remnant → 余民（罗马书 9:27）；miracles → 异能（哥林多前书 12:10）；authority → 权柄（马太福音 28:18）；faith → 信心（约翰一书 5:4）；Redeemer → 救赎主（诗篇 19:14）。

- enemy → **仇敌**（可能相关，先辨义，马太福音 13:39）。属灵争战或教训中可用“仇敌”。是否特指魔鬼须有依据；爱仇敌的诫命也可指人。

- heal → **医治**（可能相关，先辨义，马太福音 10:8）。分清施行医治与领受医治；若原文说伤口自然痊愈，照实译。

## 和合本参照

译名只供辨明为同一人物或地点时采用。同节经文或英文措辞重合只供核对；保留作者改动的受词、人称与条件。已评阅的本书译法优先。

- Christ → 基督（称号，马太福音 1:1）

- Haman → 哈曼（人名，以斯帖记 3:1）

- Jesus → 耶稣（人名，马太福音 1:1）

- Mark → 马可（人名，使徒行传 12:12）

马可福音 6:7　耶稣叫了十二个门徒来，差遣他们两个两个地出去，也赐给他们权柄，制伏污鬼；

## 本篇句法复核

- 「will be revealed」　原文是否说明谁做了这件事？中文可换语序，施事和受事各要有据。
- 「give you」　谁向谁求、谁赐给谁、益处归谁？把英文介词链改成中文动词。
- 「My children」　这是称呼，还是句中的第三人称？从邻句确认“你”“你们”及“我”的所指。

## 复核与交付

请先据本包起草，逐段回看命中的惯用词。定稿时用 check_terms.py 将本包与中文稿对读，逐条决定采用或保留异译，再把定稿并入合订本。网页版模型可由制包者代跑，将待核清单交回同一模型。

遮住英文，按说话者的口吻通读全段。如果搭配拗口或要读第二遍才知道谁在做什么，就调整语序、停顿或动词。再对照英文核实人称、意象与应许的范围，只交付定稿。保留标题、日期、段落、编号和引经标注。影响理解时才加简短译者注。重大疑错只有在上下文足够明确时才修正，并在本篇末尾写“原文修正说明”；重大歧义无法解决时保留原意，在本篇末尾写“译者待核实问题”。不写到合订本末尾；术语说明可用“译者注”。

# Chapter 133 · 2026-11-23 · More of Me

11-23-26

-MORE OF ME-

My children, this is the season of change. Things will definitely change. You are moving into a new era. A new system that is free of this evil. All things work for good to those who are in Christ Jesus. A new day is dawning. You are starting over. Your new adventure is just starting. It’s right up ahead but first there are a few things that need to be dealt with. A perverse and evil nephilim and all those who serve them. The day of vengeance has arrived. You will move forward after they are gone and take possession of all My promises. The Golden Age, a land of milk and honey. A place for My righteous to reign and rule. You have the anointing on this earth. It’s time for your anointing to fall. Fall like rain in this season.

A multitude of disasters are about to strike. A bomb threat, a missile, a deadly virus and more. You are locked in safe in the land of Goshen. This will all boomerang on them. They are in for a rude awakening. The days of Haman are here. Time to get going. The set time is now. You are protected. You wear My crown.

A sacred hiding place I have for you. You have nothing to fear. You are prepared, armored up in praise and worship. This is what destroys the enemy. No fear allowed! Keep marching through to the other side. An American revolution is taking place. This is a high stakes game but you are the winner. This is where the forever loser takes his mortal blow. It’s all over for him.

You stand tall and proud. You are My ambassadors in this hour of trial upon the earth. You wear My armor. You are clothed in righteousness, set apart for this very time. You are the ones who lead the way. I am setting captives free and you make a difference. Your knowledge of all I have revealed are your weapons and tools to heal. You are My chosen remnant I chose to change the world. Your anointing will be revealed soon!

An outpouring of My Spirit and glory are about to fall. Imagine what that will feel like. Liquid love filling you full. A love so genuine and true. A power from on high. A place where you ask and receive. A place where you will shine of My glory. People will follow where ever you go. You will be full and overflowing. Expect miracles, signs and wonders! Walk in your authority! It’s yours for the taking.

As for the haters and backbiters, I will deal with them. Just commit your ways to Me. A force like the world has never seen! A spiritual war in the heavenly’s pouring on to earth. The war of the ages is here! Victory is yours so sing and shout. The enemy is down for the count. You are a holy generation who wear My crown. I have it all to give you now!

Planes will be grounded this week. This Thanksgiving you have so to experience. A change of the current system. A market crash, a change in the monetary ways. A filter put in place to never allow this again.

You are moving on to higher ground. Expect big things from now on. You are crossing over your Red Sea now. A life of freedom is here! Declare your victory! March on through. It’s time to take what is yours!

A bullet proof vest, a failed attempt. They are caught red-handed. The wolf will be exposed in this hour.

Hold on tight, you are in for the ride of your lives. Trust in all I am doing! Have faith and lead others through. This is the moment you’ve waited for says the LORD of HOST your Redeemer!

“And He called to Him the Twelve [apostles] and began to send them out [as His ambassadors] two by two and gave them authority and power over the unclean spirits.”

Mark 6:7 AMPC
